The Unplugged Raagam spirit

A safe and judgement free singalong and jamming community for singers of all levels. Bring your voice, a favourite line, or just listen—lyrics on screen, tea on the side, no audition.

Lasya and Pranidhi founded Unplugged Raagam and have hosted 20+ events with a community that keeps growing.

Read our hosts' note

Dear singer,

We are Lasya and Pranidhi, hosts and founders of Unplugged Raagam. We are a team of two who are passionate about music and want to create a safe and judgement free space for singers to perform and sing along.

We have successfully hosted more than 20 events to date and have a strong community of singers who sing together and jam after.

We are always looking for new singers to join our community and perform with us. We are also looking for new venues to host our events.

Lasya and Pranidhi · Hosts & founders
